Accessibility statement

Last reviewed: 25 November 2026

Our commitment

We want the E-MORE website to be usable by everyone. We design it to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

Current status

This is a self-assessment; the website has not been audited or certified by an external body. We believe it meets WCAG 2.2 AA for the checks below, with the limitations listed further down.

How it was checked

  • Automated tests with axe-core on every page.
  • Calculation of colour contrast for all text and interface colours, in the default and in the high-contrast display.
  • Manual review of structure: headings, landmarks, links, images and keyboard navigation.

What you can do on this site

  • Display options (button “Accessibility” in the header): high contrast, larger text, wider text spacing and underlined links. Your choice is remembered in your browser.
  • High contrast turns on automatically if your system asks for more contrast.
  • Skip link to jump to the main content, and a visible focus indicator for keyboard use.
  • All pages work with the keyboard alone, with browser zoom up to 200 % and on small screens.
  • Images and diagrams have text descriptions. No content moves or plays automatically.

Known limitations

  • We have not yet tested with screen readers on every platform.
  • Diagrams are described in text, but the description is a summary of what they show.
  • The names of some institutions and calls are in Spanish; text is marked with its language, but image descriptions are not.

Report a problem

If you find a barrier or need information in another format, please tell us by opening an issue at the project’s issue tracker on GitHub. Describe the page and the difficulty you had.
