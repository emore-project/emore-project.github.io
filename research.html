Research

The scientific lines of the project. More detail will be added as the work advances.

Efficient adaptation

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning, quantization and cross-modal transfer to specialize large models at a small fraction of the cost.

Multimodal distillation

Transferring the knowledge of very large multimodal models into compact students that stay robust and adaptable.

Reinforcement learning for adaptation

Learning to prompt and adapt multimodal systems, with reward models that take several modalities into account.

Structured representations

Geometric and neurosymbolic representations that capture hierarchy and meaning beyond flat embeddings.

Trustworthy multimodal AI

Unlearning, privacy and auditability for multimodal models, so that they can be used responsibly.
