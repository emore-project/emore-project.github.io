Institutions and funding bodies behind E-MORE.

Funded by

E-MORE is a project of the call “Proyectos de Generación de Conocimiento 2025”, funded by the Spanish Ministry of Science, Innovation and Universities and the State Research Agency, and co-funded by the European Union through the European Social Fund Plus (ESF+). It is hosted by the Universitat de Barcelona.
